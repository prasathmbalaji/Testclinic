Patient information

Useful information to help you contact the clinic and prepare for a consultation.

Contact the clinic

Call the clinic or send a neutral WhatsApp enquiry to ask about available consultation slots.

2

Confirm your appointment

Clinic staff should confirm the date, time, consultation fee and any instructions directly.

3

Prepare for your visit

4

Discuss follow-up

Ask the doctor about follow-up arrangements and any next steps relevant to your care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

How can I enquire about an appointment?

Call +91 77088 04466 or use the appointment enquiry page. The clinic must confirm availability.

What are the clinic timings?

Public listings indicate evening consultations Monday to Saturday. Please call the clinic to confirm current timings before visiting.

What is the consultation fee?

A third-party listing has shown ₹400. Confirm the current fee directly with the clinic before your visit.

Is an online enquiry a confirmed appointment?

No. An enquiry is only a request. The clinic must confirm the appointment directly.
